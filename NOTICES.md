### Usage notice for the CC0 assets

CC0 removes the kit author's own claims; it cannot remove the terms of the models that made the audio. Those terms (the Apache-2.0 licence of Qwen3-TTS and the MIT licence of ACE-Step, plus anything the model providers say about generated audio, which is UNVERIFIED above) are the user's to check. If your needs differ, you do not have to use these assets:

- make a different house voice by describing one to VoiceDesign (see `video/voice/house/`),
- generate your own bed with `setup.sh --music`, or
- bring your own licensed track and point `video/score/score.json` at it.
